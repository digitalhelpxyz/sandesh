Delete Account

This page is for users of Sandesh Messenger who want to request deletion of their app account and associated data.

Important: After account deletion, your profile information, phone number, user ID, messages, and other associated data may be permanently deleted where technically possible, except data that must be retained for legal, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution purposes.

Request Account or Data Deletion

What Data May Be Deleted

  • Account profile information
  • Registered phone number
  • User ID or account identifier
  • Messages and other in-app content, where technically possible
  • App activity and related user-generated data

Processing Time

We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days. Some data may be retained if required by law, security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, or legitimate business purposes.
