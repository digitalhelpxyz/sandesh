Delete Your Account - Sandesh Messenger

```

If you want to delete your account and associated data from Sandesh Messenger, follow the steps below:

Steps to Delete Account

  • Open the Sandesh Messenger app
  • Go to Settings
  • Tap on "Delete Account"

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by contacting us:

Email: digitalhelpxyz@gmail.com

Data Deletion

When you request account deletion, the following data will be deleted:

  • Your phone number
  • Your profile information
  • Your messages and chat history

Data Retention

All data will be permanently deleted within 7 days of your request.
```
