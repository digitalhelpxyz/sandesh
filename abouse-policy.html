Abuse Policy

Effective Date: May 2, 2026

This Abuse Policy applies to Sandesh Messenger, provided by WebSphare. We want Sandesh Messenger to be a safe, respectful, and reliable communication platform for all users.

Prohibited Use

You must not use Sandesh Messenger to create, send, share, promote, or support:

  • Illegal content or activity
  • Harassment, bullying, threats, intimidation, or abuse
  • Hate speech or content targeting people based on protected characteristics
  • Violence, terrorism, extremism, or content encouraging physical harm
  • Sexual exploitation, child sexual abuse material, or content involving minors in an unsafe manner
  • Spam, bulk messaging, automated messaging, or unwanted promotional messages
  • Fraud, scams, phishing, fake offers, or impersonation
  • Malware, hacking attempts, or attempts to damage devices, accounts, or services
  • False, misleading, or deceptive content intended to harm users or the public
  • Content that violates intellectual property, privacy, or legal rights of others

Messaging Abuse

Users must not misuse Sandesh Messenger messaging features. Prohibited messaging behavior includes:

  • Sending repeated unwanted messages
  • Using automated tools or bots to message users
  • Creating fake accounts for spam or abuse
  • Sharing harmful links or suspicious files
  • Attempting to collect private information from other users without permission

User Responsibility

You are responsible for the messages, media, and other content you send or share through Sandesh Messenger. You must ensure that your use of the Application complies with applicable laws and our Terms of Service.

If you experience or notice abuse, spam, harassment, fraud, or any policy violation on Sandesh Messenger, please report it to us.

Action We May Take

After reviewing an abuse report or detecting misuse, we may take appropriate action, including but not limited to:

  • Warning the user
  • Restricting account features
  • Temporarily suspending an account
  • Permanently terminating an account
  • Removing or limiting access to abusive content where technically possible
  • Reporting serious illegal activity to law enforcement or relevant authorities

Law Enforcement and Safety

We may cooperate with law enforcement agencies or government authorities when required by applicable law, legal process, or when necessary to protect users, the public, or the security of Sandesh Messenger.

No Tolerance for Child Safety Violations

Sandesh Messenger has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material, exploitation of minors, grooming, or any content that puts children at risk. Such content may result in immediate account termination and reporting to appropriate authorities.
